About Focus Points

Focus Points uses all of your typing so far, not just your last block. It shows the keys and letter pairs that take you longest to reach, which is what your next blocks will practice.

The Results screen, by contrast, only covers the block you just finished.

      How scoring works

      Your score out of 100 blends three things:

      • 45%Accuracy: how many keys you hit correctly.
      • 35%Speed: your WPM, capped at 100.
      • 20%Rhythm: how steady your pace is.

      A rhythm spike is a pause between two letters that's much longer than your usual pace. Each spike costs 1.2 points, up to 20 in total.
